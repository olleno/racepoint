Your page on Race Point already exists — it is built from the official FIS points list. Here you add what is yours: social media, your own site and your sponsors. It costs nothing.

Why it is worth a quarter of an hour

  1. Your sponsors are seen where people actually look. Your page is found by everyone who searches your name or follows your results. A sponsor logo there is worth more than one in an Instagram story that is gone the next day.
  2. You become searchable as yourself. Today FIS has you as a number and a list of points. Here you exist with your own channels and your own story.
  3. You get your own link to share. If you share it and someone buys something through it, 80 per cent goes to you — paid to your club and earmarked for you. 10 per cent goes to the Race Point Foundation, which awards grants, and 10 per cent to running the site.
  4. We are negotiating with larger partners. The aim is our own agreements with services ski racers actually use — currency cards, travel insurance, eSIM — where you earn when someone signs up through your link.

Honestly, where this stands: the links and the sponsors work today. The affiliate programme and the support button are still being built and open during the season. Tick the boxes below and we will let you know when they do — you are not committing to anything.
